Caught this stunning sunset at Santa Monica Beach during golden hour. The warm light reflecting off the water created perfect conditions for landscape photography.

The combination of low ISO and a medium aperture gave me the sharpness I was looking for while maintaining good depth of field across the entire scene.

This is one of my favorite spots for sunset photography - the pier provides interesting foreground elements and the west-facing beach ensures spectacular light during the evening hours.
