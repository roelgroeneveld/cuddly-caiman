# About This Site

Welcome! This is a personal blog and photo site built with [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/), a simple and powerful static site generator.

## What You'll Find Here

### Blog
I write two types of blog posts:

- **Regular posts**: Longer-form articles with titles on various topics
- **Micro posts**: Quick thoughts and notes without titles, similar to social media posts

### Photo Blog
A collection of photographs with detailed metadata, including camera settings and location information. Click any photo to view it in high resolution with zoom capability.

## About the Technology

This site is built with:
- **Eleventy**: Static site generator
- **PhotoSwipe**: For photo galleries and zoom functionality
- **Markdown**: For writing content

## Features

- **Responsive design**: Works on all devices
- **Fast loading**: Static HTML with optimized images
- **Photo zoom**: Click any photo to view in full resolution
- **No tracking**: Privacy-focused, no analytics or cookies
